## Scope

A command with which a role enrolls itself in the work protocol from its own current state, without a hand-written enrollment input file.

## Criteria

- It derives the context and inspection receipt from the role's own journal, registry binding and canonical references.
- It publishes through the existing enrollment producer and refuses any role other than the caller's own.
- The enrollment receipt format does not change.

## Decisions

- The caller's own role is the registered role whose effective registry binding equals the
  supplied `--native-id` or the harness's session variable, that supported binding resolution
  accepts and that the journal already holds. The native ID is compared, not authenticated, and
  the documentation states those limits.
- An unclaimed journal is claimed at epoch 1; a journal held under another binding is refused, so
  fencing a prior writer stays an explicit `protocol claim`.
- Every known reference is listed and each required scope becomes an UNKNOWN with a derived
  inspection artifact as evidence, so the receipt is adopted without a hand-written input.
- Registered roles only; workers keep `protocol enroll` with their verified lineage census.

## Outcome

`protocol enroll-self --role ROLE --at ISO_TIMESTAMP` replaces the claim, report, artifact,
revision and input-file sequence with one command that publishes through `EnrollmentStore` and
writes the existing receipt format.
