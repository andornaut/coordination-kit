## Scope

Keep message status, dispatch and the channel consumer's guard bounded as the message store grows.

Baseline on delivery-kit 4.6.2 at about 2650 records (73 MB): `protocol message-receipt` about 50 s
per receipt, `message status --recipient` several minutes, `message status --id` and
`message reconcile` about 1 s.

## Criteria

- The records read per operation do not grow with the total number of stored messages, including `protocol message-receipt`.
- Existing message records remain readable byte for byte.
- Status output and exit codes do not change.
