## Scope

Retry a delivery refused as transport-busy at the next boundary, without operator action.

## Criteria

- Only a refusal proven to occur before native admission is retried.
- An ambiguous or unknown outcome is never retried.
- Each attempt keeps its own bound receipt.

## Decisions

- The retry runs within the same `message dispatch`, after a one-second wait, so it needs no
  operator action, timer or later boundary.
- Proof of pre-admission refusal: the attempt this dispatch added completed as
  `transport-busy`, and no receipt exists at its bound path. The Claude spool refuses before
  writing a receipt or spool entry.
- The retry is a full guarded dispatch: ownership, routing, decision clearance and readiness
  are rechecked, and a new attempt takes its own request ID and receipt path.
- At most one retry. Ambiguous, lost, refused, unavailable and receipt-bearing outcomes return
  unchanged, and a second busy refusal stays `blocked` for a later dispatch.

## Outcome

Delivered. Tests cover a busy refusal retried into acceptance, the single-retry bound, guards
rechecked before the retry, and no retry for ambiguous, lost, refused, unavailable or
receipt-bearing outcomes.
