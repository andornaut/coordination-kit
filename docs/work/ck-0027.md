## Scope

Move canonical coordination to ignored `var/coordination/` in the primary coordinator checkout.
Preserve existing CLI consumers, runner cadence and the external SQLite store until qualification
and the coordinator's fenced switch.

## Criteria

- Store immutable sender originals by exact ID and recipient-owned disposition records within
  registered role compartments. Use verified worker lineage for distinct worker namespaces.
- Keep a durable append-only audit history and rebuildable indexes. Serialize policy holds, work
  starts and multi-record transitions with bounded recovery and deduplication.
- Preserve caller bindings, native guards, handoff attestation, pending questions, work evidence,
  delivery cursors and unknowns. Scope coordinator-issued user holds without changing other holds.
- Qualify a consistent SQLite snapshot, full state comparison, independent backup and restore,
  compatible deployment and one authority switch without dual writes.
