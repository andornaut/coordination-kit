## Scope

Unify Codex endpoint discovery, expose the latest wake outcome and next safe action by original
ID, and make unread work visible independently of guarded turn delivery. Reconcile native
question identities in the existing durable ledger and fence only work dependent on an answer.

## Criteria

- Preserve real prompts, unknowns, drafts, compaction ambiguity, independent holds and Claude.
- Preserve original message IDs, guarded no-retry semantics and exact recipient collection.
- Verify recovery across reconnect or compaction through supported client capabilities. Surface
  exact recorded question and unread evidence through status when the original form is unavailable.
- Verify the published source through a representative live send, recipient pull and handling path.

## Outcome

The [v3.3.0 release](https://github.com/andornaut/coordination-kit/releases/tag/v3.3.0) passed 470
unit and 11 contract tests, plus both exact GitHub workflows. The coordinator adopted the checked
digest and uses the shared Codex control socket resolver for startup. With the override unset,
installed readiness and doctor reached the managed endpoint and observed the exact role thread.

Live qualification followed two post-adoption originals from send through recipient collection and
handling. One wake was accepted. The other was refused at decision clearance and appeared in the
recipient's read-only inbox before delivery. The first inbox pull collected the original once and
the second collected zero. Status also showed the older Chibi D9 exact original as unread with
its historical wake evidence unavailable and its next owner action intact.

Codex 0.171.0 exposes the native request and its identifiers through the app-server protocol.
Its resolution notification covers both a client response and turn cleanup. The native queue
accepts user input, so adding a queue entry cannot serve as a passive unread alert. Restoring
the same pending blocking form and recovering its exact disposition remain unverified through
the available client operations. The existing status and review report the recorded question,
ordered options and owning thread while native state remains unresolved. See the
