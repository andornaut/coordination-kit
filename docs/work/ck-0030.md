Apply the 4 GiB ceiling to the kit's check, release and release-cache containers. Preserve the
existing CPU limit, image pins, checkout isolation and published artifact format. Verify the
cap in the launched release container.

## Outcome

The Makefile, offline pack controller and CI release-cache step use a fixed 4 GiB limit.
