Give the coordinator early visibility into confirmed unread unblock wake failures. Provide a
registry-fenced batch and durable attempt receipt for the existing coordinator review and
event boundaries. Keep original message disposition independent from a reminder. Preserve
holds, pending questions, drafts, and uncertain wake outcomes. Verify that repeated sends,
overlapping reviews, and ambiguous input do not duplicate reminders.
