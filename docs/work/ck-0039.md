Give Game Coordinator a manual writer reservation before preparing a checkpoint. Acquire it
after current writers drain, bind it to the exact journal head and registry revision, and
defer writes under the same lock, including from preopened writers. Keep reads available and
keep the prepare receipt and approval evidence outside the reserved journal.

Qualify cancellation, concurrent writers, stable review and runner event state, retry and
interrupted activation against an independent restore. Game Coordinator owns live custody
and approval of any new exact receipt.

## Outcome

Coordination Kit 4.1.0 reserves the exact journal head while read-only operations continue.
Each prepared receipt binds the acquisition, evidence and independent restore. Cancellation
invalidates the acquisition's receipts. After permanent fencing, the exact activation can be
retried. Review and event ingest report a deferred write without advancing their uncommitted
state. The live runbook requires older writers to be parked, in-flight work to drain and
registry routing to stay stable through activation or retry.
