status: approved
approved: user, fix the writer and add diagnostics

related: [ck-0029, ck-0038]

Report the required fields missing or malformed in a recipient's decision marker without
exposing values. Preserve the existing refusal for invalid or pending markers and exact
native evidence requirements for clearance.

Identify the producer of the incomplete marker. Add prevention at its verified source or
hand off exact evidence to the producer's owner. The recipient retains custody of live
marker correction and verifies native evidence before changing it.
