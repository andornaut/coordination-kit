Question ownership is by registry role on Claude and Codex. A rotated or rehomed binding of the
same role answers, reconciles and cancels its own questions.

- Remove the Codex thread check from answer and reconcile and the native-proof requirement from
  cancel. Attach and reconcile remain optional evidence.
- Accept `--depends-on` on Claude, as on Codex.
- Add a one-time close for questions left pending from the Codex period, citing an approval
  reference as policy holds do, marked CLEANUP.
- One pending question cannot silence its role's wakes indefinitely.

Source request: Game Coordinator, package B and the post-rotation question-close defect.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-25; answers q-ck-packages-ab): Yes, both: B first, then A (Recommended)

## Outcome

Question ownership is by registry role on both harnesses. A rotated or rehomed binding of the
owning role answers, cancels, attaches and reconciles its questions; another role is still
`not-owner`. Answer and cancel no longer read or require native evidence, and `--depends-on` is
accepted on Claude. `question close-legacy --id ID --reason TEXT --approval REF` closes a pending
question opened under a Codex binding, recorded as `question.cancelled` with the approval
reference, and is marked CLEANUP. A pending question withholds send-time wakes only; the
scheduled review wakes past it, so it delays a wake by at most one review slot. New
`question.opened` events record the opener's `harness`; no event kind is added. Released in 5.2.0.
