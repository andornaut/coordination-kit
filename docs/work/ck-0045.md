Wakes reach a role's current binding, including after a Claude `/clear` rebind.

- The Claude channel resolves the role's binding on each poll instead of fixing the
  conversation ID at start.
- At review time, send one native wake per recipient with unread originals, through one harness
  adapter: Claude through its channel or native cross-session message, Codex through the control
  socket. This rearm path replaces the reminder claim and settle ledger and the urgent-attention
  messages, which are deleted. The store question guard replaces the decision-marker clearance.
- The brief prints the native-visibility line only when a supported survey exists, groups stalled
  and overdue exceptions per recipient (count, oldest ID, age) and keeps the full IDs in
  `review show`.

Source request: Game Coordinator, package A.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-25; answers q-ck-packages-ab): Yes, both: B first, then A (Recommended)

## Outcome

Claude wake files are keyed by role (`wake/roles/<role>.json`), so the running channel keeps
announcing a role's wakes after `session start` rebinds it after `/clear`; with `--conversation`
the channel resolves its role once at its first poll. Send time and review time share one wake
path with one adapter per harness: the Claude wake file, or one guarded Codex queue add that
checks native input state and adds no wake while an earlier one is still queued. The scheduled
review rearms and wakes every recipient with unread originals once per slot, past a pending
store question but not past role or policy holds. The reminder claim and settle ledger, its
`reminder` verbs, the coordinator attention messages, the native notice proofs, the Codex
decision-marker clearance and the `decision marker` and `decision resolve` verbs are removed.
The review and `question reconcile` still read an existing marker read-only. Journals holding the
retired event kinds replay and rebuild, and compaction still qualifies them. The brief prints the
native prompt visibility lines only when a supported survey ran, and groups stalled and overdue
originals per recipient with count, oldest ID and age; `review show` keeps every ID. Released in
5.3.0.
