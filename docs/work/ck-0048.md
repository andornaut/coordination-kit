`import-x/no-cycle` over `src/`, `scripts/` and `tests/`, and `no-restricted-imports`

## Outcome

ESLint guards imports as in the games: `import-x/no-cycle` 4.18.1 rejects runtime cycles over
`src/`, `scripts/` and `tests/`, and one `no-restricted-imports` boundary per `src/` layer folder
also rejects upward type-only imports and re-exports; type-only cycles within a layer are allowed.
Moving `Command` and `Implementation` to `tests/contract/lib/implementation.ts` removed the
type-only `adapter.ts` and `node-adapter.ts` cycle.
