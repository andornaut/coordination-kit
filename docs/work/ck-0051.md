A channel launched with a conversation that a `/clear` rotated follows the lineage to the role's
current binding. The successor transcript can sit in a different Claude project directory from
its predecessor when the session's working directory is reached through another path, such as a
symlink. The successor search then never finds it, and the channel stays unresolved.

- The successor search covers every project directory, as the predecessor search does, and keeps
  its header limit and once-a-minute rate.
- The review reports a Claude role whose channel status file is missing or names a conversation
  other than its binding.

Source request: Game Coordinator review defect report.

- D1 (user, 2026-11-25; answers q-ck-channel-lineage): Yes, as 6.4.0 before 7.0.0 (Recommended)
